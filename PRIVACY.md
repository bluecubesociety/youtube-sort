# Privacy Policy

**YouTube Sort** does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

## What is stored

- **Video metadata** (title, channel, duration, views, upload date) is read from the YouTube page you are visiting and stored locally in your browser using `browser.storage.local`. This data never leaves your device.
- **Extension settings** (sort order, filter preferences) are stored using `browser.storage.sync`, which may be synced across your own devices by your browser if you have sync enabled. No third party has access to this data.

## What is not stored

- No browsing history is recorded beyond the YouTube tabs currently open.
- No account information, cookies, or credentials are accessed or stored.
- No analytics or tracking of any kind is performed.
- No data is sent to any external server.

If you have any questions, please open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/bluecubesociety/youtube-sort/issues) or visit [bluecubesociety.com](https://bluecubesociety.com/).
